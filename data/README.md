HF radar viewer bundles (`hfr_YYYYMM.js`) and their index (`hfr_manifest.js`), produced by
`tools/process_all_hfr.py`. The viewer loads a month only when the model clock reaches it.
Analysis files (`.npz`) are written to `analysis/npz/` and are not needed by the website.
These are GISTDA observations: check the data-sharing policy before publishing the repository.
